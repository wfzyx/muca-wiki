# Ancient Sets — Adamantine

Classe: **Dark Lord**. Os dois Ancients deste set base:

![Adamantine](/img/sets/adamantine.jpg)

- **Bônus parciais:**
  - 2: Double Damage Chance +10%
  - 3: Defense +40
  - 4: Skill Damage +20
- **Full set:** Critical DMG Chance +15%, Excellent DMG Chance +15%, Critical Damage +20, Excellent Damage +20.

- **Bônus parciais:**

  - 3: Skill Damage +20

- **Full set:** Critical DMG Chance +15%, Excellent DMG Chance +15%, Ignore Defense Chance +5%, Leadership +30.
