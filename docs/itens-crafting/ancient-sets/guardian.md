# Ancient Sets — Guardian

Classe: **Fairy Elf**. Os dois Ancients deste set base:

![Guardian](/img/sets/guardian.jpg)

## Gywen Aruan — `/Aruan`
- **Peças:** Silver Bow, Guardian Armor/Gloves/Boots, Pendant of Ability.
- **Bônus parciais:**
  - 2: Agility +30
  - 3: Min Attack Damage +20
  - 4: Defense +20
  - 5: Max Attack Damage +20
- **Full set:** Critical DMG Chance +15%, Excellent DMG Chance +15%, Critical Damage +20, Excellent Damage +20.

- **Peças:** Guardian Helm/Armor/Pants/Boots.
- **Bônus parciais:**

  - 3: Double Damage Chance +10%
  - 4: Skill Damage +20
- **Full set:** Critical DMG Chance +15%, Excellent DMG Chance +15%, Ignore Defense Chance +5%.
